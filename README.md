### Hi, I'm Valentin 👋

I like to build open tools that help researchers monitor insects and other wildlife with affordable smartphones and computer vision. 
I'm a postdoc at [UFZ](https://www.ufz.de/) and [iDiv](https://www.idiv.de/) in Leipzig, Germany.

**Selected projects**
- [FaunaPulse](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/fauna-pulse): an Android app that turns a phone into an AI wildlife camera, with detection, tracking and identification running offline on the device.
  (Also on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.faunapulse.app))
- [FaunaLapse](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/fauna-lapse): turns old or inexpensive phones into screen-off time-lapse cameras for watching insects on flowers.
- [smartphone-insect-detect](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/smartphone-insect-detect): how well pretrained YOLO detectors find pollinators in unseen time-lapse images ([Scientific Reports, 2025](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-025-16140-z))
- [plotbiomes](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/plotbiomes): Whittaker biome diagrams with ggplot2 (R)
- [bootstrapnet](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/bootstrapnet): bootstrapping indices of bipartite ecological networks (R)
- [geobuffer](https://github.com/valentinitnelav/geobuffer): geodesic buffers with radii in metres (R)
